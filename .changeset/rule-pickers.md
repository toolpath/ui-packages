Draw the rule list's pickers — a rule's features, measure and comparisons, and "+ New Rule" — as `@toolpath/ui` menus in place of native `<select>`s, so their open lists take the kit's popover, items and dark theme rather than the operating system's. Each word is a button named for its field and value, "Measure: any size"; its menu opens on the value it holds, and "More…" lists the rest in the open menu.
